Xunchen Liu | Laser Diagnostics Laboratory, Shandong University of Science and Technology

We develop non-intrusive laser diagnostics — TDLAS, PIV, PLIF and Rayleigh scattering — to image flames and combustion. These methods offer high spatio-temporal resolution and on-line, multi-parameter field measurements, and are widely applied to engine combustion diagnostics.
